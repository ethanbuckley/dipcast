dipcast

Will sewage from upstream storm overflows reach this swim spot?

Click a river or lake on the map. Storm overflows appear as dots when you zoom in: discharging now not discharging offline / no live feed

Covers all nine English water companies' live overflow status. Welsh Water areas show annual spill history only.

Risk is the modelled chance that at least one upstream spill reaches this point after travel time, bacterial die-off and dilution. It is a forecast, not a measurement: a "low" reading is not a guarantee of clean water, and the model knows nothing about farm runoff, treated effluent or algae. How accurate is it?
