Terms of use

Plain-English terms for using Dipspot. Last updated 29 September 2026.

What Dipspot is

Dipspot is a free, experimental tool that estimates the chance that sewage from upstream storm overflows affects a point on a river or lake in England, now and over the coming days, and on rivers the chance that a water sample would fail the E. coli limit for bathing water. It combines water companies' published overflow data, rainfall forecasts, river-network geometry and statistical models, and repeats the Environment Agency's rating of designated bathing waters. It is a forecast, not a measurement.

What it is not

Dipspot does not test water. Its spill forecast covers monitored storm overflows only. Its E. coli estimate allows for rain washing in pollution from other sources on average, but knows nothing about a particular farm, road, boat, outfall or incident. At designated bathing waters the site also repeats the Environment Agency's yearly rating and its sampler's latest visual algae check; those are the Environment Agency's, dated, not Dipspot forecasts, and the algae check does not tell harmful algae from harmless kinds. The site does not show the Environment Agency's short-term advice against bathing after an incident: check its bathing-water pages. A "low" reading means nothing the site looks at flags the day; it does not mean the water is clean or safe, and it says nothing about currents, cold water or other hazards. A "high" reading is a probability, not a confirmed spill.

Nothing on this site is advice about your health or safety. Whether to enter the water is your decision. If you are unwell after swimming, seek medical advice. For official water-quality information use the Environment Agency's bathing-water service and any local notices.

Accuracy

The model's skill is published on the verification page, including where it is wrong. Overflow status can be delayed, missing or incorrect at source; some overflows have no live monitor; rainfall forecasts have error that grows with lead time; the river network and outfall positions are approximate. Expect the forecast to be wrong some of the time.
