Will sewage from upstream storm overflows reach this swim spot?

Overflow exposure is the modelled chance that at least one upstream storm overflow spills and its discharge reaches the spot, after travel time, bacterial die-off and dilution. The spill part is verified against what the overflows did; the die-off and dilution weights are physical estimates, not calibrated against water samples, so treat it as an index of exposure rather than a measured probability. E. coli > 900 is the modelled chance a water sample would fail the inland "sufficient" standard, fitted to 1,750 Environment Agency samples at 32 bathing waters from rainfall at the spot, overflow exposure, water-body type and season, tested year-out. Forecasts, not measurements: the model knows nothing about a specific farm, outfall or algal bloom, and "low" is not a guarantee of clean water. Inclusion of a spot is not a statement that it is safe to swim there. How accurate is it?
